## Status vocabulary

- current: verified against the current source
- changed: source changed and the topic needs review
- historical: retained for change history
- partial: only part of the source was accessible or reviewed
- blocked: access was unavailable
- unknown: not yet assessed

A snapshot is evidence of what was reviewed at that time, not a claim that the live source is unchanged.
