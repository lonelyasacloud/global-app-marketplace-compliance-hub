# Shared Controls / 共性合规控制

这些主题可能同时影响 Apple App Store、Google Play 和当地法律，但具体平台义务仍需分别核对。

## Core topics / 核心主题

- Privacy and data protection / 隐私与数据保护：[shared baseline](privacy-data-protection/privacy-baseline.zh-CN.md) · [English](privacy-data-protection/privacy-baseline.en-US.md). Apple App Privacy Details, Privacy Manifest, Required Reason API, and ATT are not separate topic pages; the 2026-11-10 snapshot did not include those full texts.
- Account creation and deletion / 账户创建与删除：Google Play [account deletion](../google-play/account-deletion/account-deletion.zh-CN.md) · [English](../google-play/account-deletion/account-deletion.en-US.md). An Apple account-deletion topic page is not in the snapshot.
- Permissions and consent / 权限与同意：Google Play [User Data](../google-play/user-data/user-data.zh-CN.md) and [sensitive permissions](../google-play/sensitive-permissions/sensitive-permissions.zh-CN.md).
- User-generated content and moderation / 用户生成内容与审核：Google Play [UGC](../google-play/user-generated-content/user-generated-content.zh-CN.md). Apple UGC is in the [guidelines summary](../apple-app-store/app-review-guidelines/guidelines-summary.zh-CN.md), not a separate children or UGC topic.
- Children and age-sensitive experiences / 儿童与年龄敏感体验：Apple [age ratings](../apple-app-store/age-ratings/age-ratings.zh-CN.md); Google Play [age restrictions and child safety](../google-play/age-restricted-child-safety/age-restricted-child-safety.zh-CN.md). A separate Apple children topic is not in the snapshot.
- Payments, subscriptions and refunds / 付款、订阅与退款：Google Play [payments](../google-play/payments/payments-and-subscriptions.zh-CN.md). Apple IAP, subscriptions, and external purchase are not topic pages; the [license summary](../apple-app-store/developer-program/license-agreement.zh-CN.md) is the agreement-level note only.
- Third-party SDK and supply-chain transparency / 第三方 SDK 与供应链透明度：Google Play [SDK requirements](../google-play/sdk-requirements/sdk-requirements.zh-CN.md).
- Security, abuse prevention and incident response / 安全、滥用防护与事件响应：Google Play [malware](../google-play/malware/malware.zh-CN.md), [device and network abuse](../google-play/device-and-network-abuse/device-and-network-abuse.zh-CN.md), and [enforcement](../google-play/enforcement-and-appeals/enforcement-and-appeals.zh-CN.md).

## Editorial rule / 编辑规则

Shared documents describe the cross-platform control objective. Platform-specific documents describe the exact Apple or Google implementation and submission requirement. Do not infer that passing one platform automatically satisfies the other.
