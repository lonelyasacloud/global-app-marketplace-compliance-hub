## Scope and declaration

Primary sources are the official [News & Magazines policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9935326) and the [requirements guide for news and magazine apps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10523915), reviewed October 22, 2026. The policy defines scope as an app declaring itself News/Magazine in Play Console, or one both listed in Play's News and Magazine category and describing itself as news/magazine in its title, icon, developer name, or description. The implementation guide separately lists prior declaration, category, and self-description characteristics, and says Console may prompt apps with any or all of them; the two pages' boundary wording is not identical. The conservative approach is to follow the live Console prompt and ask official support when uncertain. Complete a separate declaration for each in-scope app and update it when information changes.

Examples include news, newspaper, magazine, breaking-news, local-news, and news/magazine aggregator apps, including AI-assisted aggregators. Examples the guide lists as out of scope include general media aggregators, media-streaming apps, apps primarily containing user-generated content such as social media, and apps primarily selling goods or services; media-streaming apps that offer programs including news are also listed as out of scope. Because category, naming, and functionality interact, use the live Console prompt and official guide when the boundary is unclear.

The declaration has been required since **August 27, 2025**. The guide says developers unable to meet the Console-specified time may request an extension through their Console profile; eligibility and the actual deadline must be checked in the account. Answers must describe the app and parent news/magazine entity and must be kept current by the developer.

### Separate declaration article: fields and relationships

The official [How to declare a news and magazine app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16189314?hl=en) article says the declaration may request contact details, the parent entity and its type, and may ask about the app's focus, affiliations, editorial guidelines, and awards. The listed entity types are public/government, commercial/private, community/not-for-profit, and other. If the app serves another news entity, declare that legal entity as appropriate. Editorial standards, memberships, and awards are possible background questions, not universal eligibility prerequisites.

The article describes an aggregator as an app whose all or majority of content comes from third parties, including AI news aggregators; these apps should clearly identify third-party sources. The contact URL must be accessible and current, easy to find in the app, and provide at least email or phone contact. Social links alone do not comply. Make a separate declaration for each app and update it after changes to the entity, content, or contact details.

The declaration article also links back to the [News & Magazines policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9935326?hl=en), its [requirements guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10523915?hl=en), and [Play Console Requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890?hl=en). The Console declaration route and a UNESCO classification link did not resolve to reviewable target URLs in this reader pass; their form fields/classification details are not claimed as publicly verified. The Console Requirements topic covers developer-account identity duties and does not replace the news declaration.

## Content, transparency, and monetization

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Article sources | Identify each article's source, including at least the original publisher or author. Where individual bylines are not customary, list the app as the original publisher. Aggregators must also explain third-party publishing sources. |
| Freshness | Do not provide static content. Apps providing current events/news are expected to update regularly and keep content no more than 30 days old. Other news/magazine apps should update regularly and keep content no more than three months old to remain in the category. Show each article's last-published date. |
| Contact | Provide clear, easy-to-find, current contact information in-app through a URL, using the same URL entered in Console. Provide at least an email or phone number; social links alone do not comply. |
| Paid content | If membership or a subscription is required, show an in-app preview before purchase. |
| Monetization | Ads and other marketing can be used, but affiliate marketing or ad revenue must not be the app's primary purpose. The primary purpose must not be to sell products/services or generate ad revenue. |
| Play declaration | Each in-scope app must complete and maintain its declaration. Information about the app and entity may be consistent with store-listing metadata. |

## Release checklist

- [ ] Review app title, icon, developer name, description, store category, and existing Console answers to determine whether each app triggers a separate declaration.
- [ ] Complete a separate declaration per in-scope app; update it after changes to content, entity, or contact details.
- [ ] Show each article's original source/byline and publication date; identify third-party publishers in aggregated content.
- [ ] Keep current-news content within 30 days and other news/magazine content within three months, with a regular update plan.
- [ ] Make in-app contact information easy to find and consistent with the Console URL; provide a preview before a paywall.
- [ ] Review ads, affiliate revenue, and the app's primary purpose to ensure advertising is not its main business purpose.
