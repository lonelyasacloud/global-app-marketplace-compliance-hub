## Value and limits of course pages

The FAQ on [Google Play Policy Academy](https://playacademy.withgoogle.com/courses/google-play-policy/) says its courses can help developers understand privacy-friendly practices, evaluate apps, and find alternative implementations. It also explicitly says that its best practices are **not exhaustive and may evolve**. Google recommends keeping the developer contact email up to date to receive policy-change notices. Email and courses are supplementary channels; verify requirements against the [Developer Program Policy](../developer-program-policy/developer-program-policy.en-US.md), the relevant policy topic, and Play Console.

Play Academy uses Exceed LMS. Public pages expose course titles, dates, duration, difficulty, descriptions, and learning objectives. Course pages commonly show “Log In To Launch”, so this index **does not claim to have read login-gated lessons, quizzes, video captions, or assignments**. A browser-fetch cache miss also does not prove a course has been retired.

### Four direct policy-course targets under Play Console features

I opened or attempted [Plan and prepare for app review times](https://playacademy.exceedlms.com/student/path/265136/activity/356107), [Manage policy violations](https://playacademy.exceedlms.com/student/path/265136/activity/356108), [Declare your use of device location data](https://playacademy.exceedlms.com/student/path/265136/activity/356109), and [Select your target audience and content settings](https://playacademy.exceedlms.com/student/path/265136/activity/356110). The first two exposed public descriptions but gate the lessons behind sign-in; the latter two returned cache misses, so their content is not inferred from the titles.

The review-times course description says, as of its March 19, 2024 publication (7 minutes, Beginner), that some categories—including apps targeting children—may receive expanded reviews, and introduces recommended processes and timelines. It does not guarantee a review duration; use current Play Console status and formal Help pages. The policy-violations course was published June 10, 2019 (5 minutes) and says only that developers should consider policy through the app lifecycle and learn next steps after a violation; see the current written [Enforcement and Appeals topic](../enforcement-and-appeals/enforcement-and-appeals.en-US.md). For location and target-audience rules, use the current [Location topic](../contacts-location-permissions/contacts-location-permissions.en-US.md), [Child Safety topic](../age-restricted-child-safety/age-restricted-child-safety.en-US.md), and official policies; the course bodies were not readable in this pass.
