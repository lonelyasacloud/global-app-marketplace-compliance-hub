## Review sign-in requirements: direct links and related pages

Reopened [Sign-in details requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15748846?hl=en) on October 24, 2026. The page body directly links to Play Console Requirements, the signed-in Console route for entering credentials, and a Play Academy course. `Prepare your app for review` (answer/9859455) is the upstream App content guide that links to this sign-in requirements page; it is not a child of it.

Review credentials must remain accessible, reusable, valid regardless of reviewer location, and continuously maintained without errors. If the normal flow uses an OTP or 2-Step Verification, provide reusable credentials that bypass the challenge and do not expire; expired passwords may prevent review and lead to rejection. Submit credentials and instructions in English. For non-alphanumeric QR codes/barcodes, upload a static URL. Give clear instructions for user-created PINs; provide every required third-party account and detailed steps; replace geographic restrictions with location-independent “master” credentials. Even if the app has no sign-in, provide instructions or access that lets reviewers fully and freely inspect content behind a subscription paywall.

## Pre-release checklist

1. Complete App content declarations from actual data flows, SDKs, ads, age audience, and features; revisit them after changes.
2. Provide reviewer credentials, authentication/region instructions, and any required demo materials; test them with a clean device or review account.
3. Keep the privacy policy, Data safety, store listing, permission declarations, audience, rating, and submitted build consistent.
4. Reserve review time. Use Managed publishing when you need to control launch timing, but it does not bypass review.
5. Preserve evidence for pre-review checks, formal review, rejection fixes, staged rollout, and full-rollout halt; use Play Console for account-specific cases.
