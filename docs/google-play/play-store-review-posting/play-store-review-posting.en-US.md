## How ratings are displayed

- App ratings and the star-distribution chart reflect the current quality represented by user reviews. Except when an app has very few ratings, the display is not simply the lifetime average; Google says this helps users understand the app's current state.
- Google also displays the lifetime review count as information about an app's usage and history.
- Ratings for books, movies, TV shows, and other non-app content use the lifetime average for that content.

This is a user-facing explanation. The page does not disclose an exact weighting formula, time window, or anti-abuse model; do not infer an internal ranking algorithm from it.

## Review visibility and personal information

- Play indicates whether a review will be public or private. Public reviews show the Google Account name, profile image, and past edits to Play users, including developers.
- Private reviews are common for early-access or beta apps and are visible only to the developer; the developer can still see the reviewer's name, image, and past edits.
- For both kinds of review, developers may also see language, country, and device information such as model and operating-system version, and may use it to reply.
- Editing a review does not automatically hide its earlier versions. Deleting the review removes the review record.

Before posting, users should consider visibility, developer-visible fields, and edit history together. This describes what the Play page tells reviewers; it does not replace Google Account privacy controls or applicable privacy law.

## Rules for user posts

Users may review things they purchased or rented on Google Play. Google says it does not pay anyone to rate or review content and expects reviewers to be honest and unbiased. Reviews should describe the reviewer's genuine experience, remain relevant to the item, and help other users. Play combines automated and human review to handle offensive or policy-violating posts. People who repeatedly or egregiously violate the rules may lose the ability to post reviews on Play.

| Category | Prohibited conduct |
| --- | --- |
| Fake or manipulative reviews | Fake, inaccurate, or duplicate reviews; multiple accounts reviewing the same item; misleading others or manipulating the rating; posting on someone else's behalf; misrepresenting identity or connection to the item. |
| Off-topic content | Solicitations, political or social commentary, or other content unrelated to the item, service, or actual consumer experience. |
| Advertising and redirection | Promoting other products or services, including affiliate or referral promotions; adding phone numbers, email addresses, or external website links. |
| Conflicts of interest | Offering or accepting money, products, or services for a review; reviewing a business's item, service, or experience when the reviewer owns or works for that business. |
| Intellectual property | Posting content that infringes another person's intellectual-property rights, including copyright. |
| Personal or confidential information | Posting information such as a physical address or driver's-license number. |
| Illegal content | Posting a review that contains or links to illegal content. |
| Explicit sexual content | Posting, promoting, or soliciting sexually explicit content; sexually depicting children or directing sexual content at children. |
| Hate and violence | Promoting, condoning, or inciting violence or hatred toward a person or group based on listed protected characteristics, including race, nationality, ethnic origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or veteran status. |
| Offensive or abusive content | Obscene, profane, or offensive language; harassment, bullying, or attacks on others. |

The page also recommends making reviews informative and specific, including both strengths and drawbacks, keeping writing readable, and treating others respectfully. These are quality suggestions, not a separate scoring formula.

## Relationship to developer policy

The user and developer rules complement one another but are not interchangeable:

- Users may not manipulate ratings with multiple accounts, false identities, or repeated reviews. Developers may not buy, fake, reward, or pressure users to provide reviews or installs.
- Users may share genuine experiences. Developer replies should address the issue instead of soliciting a higher rating or offering an incentive.
- This page prohibits advertising, affiliate/referral content, and off-site contact details in reviews. Developer marketing rules separately govern store listings, promotions, and review-acquisition practices.

See the [developer Ratings, Reviews, and Installs Policy](../user-ratings-reviews-installs/user-ratings-reviews-installs.en-US.md) for the separate developer obligations.
