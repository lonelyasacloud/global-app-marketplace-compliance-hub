## Listing copy: accurate, concise, and verifiable

- An **app title** may contain up to 30 characters. It should be distinctive and accurately describe the app. The title, developer name, and icon must not imply an authorization, affiliation, or official status that does not exist.
- A **short description** may contain up to 80 characters and should communicate the app's core purpose and value. A **full description** may contain up to 4,000 characters. Do not repeat the short description in the full one or use keyword lists, unrelated word blocks, excessive repetition, unclear formatting, or impossible feature claims.
- Focus on the app's real functionality and distinctive value. Attribute endorsements or quotations to a clear source; anonymous or unattributed testimonials and unsupported claims such as “best” or “number one” are not allowed.
- Titles, descriptions, images, and videos must not advertise prices, discounts, limited-time deals, rankings, awards, or download counts. Avoid all caps, irrelevant emoji, and repeated special characters used to attract attention. All localized listings remain subject to the Metadata and other policies and must describe what users actually get in that market.
- Store listings should be suitable for general audiences. If artwork or copy may appeal to children, the target-audience declaration must match. A low content rating does not replace Families, privacy, or age-gating obligations.

## Preview assets: required items and recommendations

Google's asset page distinguishes **requirements for publishing** from **recommendations** that can affect promotion and display eligibility. Violating requirements may lead to asset removal or app suspension; failing a recommendation generally does not remove the listing but may affect how assets appear or promotional opportunities.

| Asset | Key requirements | Recommendations and policy boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | 32-bit PNG with alpha; 512 × 512 px; maximum 1,024 KB. | No ranking, price, Play category badge, or misleading symbol; follow Android/Play icon specifications. |
| Short description | Required; maximum 80 characters. | Explain the core function in simple language. Avoid keyword stuffing, repetition, price/promotional or ranking/award claims, download/install calls to action, unrelated symbols, and time-sensitive claims; localize by market. |
| Feature graphic | Required; JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; 1,024 × 500 px. | Center the focal content and keep it clear of crop zones. Avoid tiny text, duplicating the app icon, unauthorized trademarks/characters, store badges, and price/ranking claims. Localize as appropriate and provide concise alt text. |
| Screenshots | Normally at least 2; JPEG or 24-bit PNG; each side 320–3,840 px; longest side no more than twice the shortest; up to 8 per device type. | Show the real app experience. Use taglines only when needed, occupying no more than 20% of the image. Avoid calls to action and ranking/award/price claims; provide alt text and localized assets. Large-screen, Wear OS, Android TV, Automotive, and XR have additional platform-specific counts, ratios, and dimensions. |
| Preview video | Optional but strongly recommended; games need one for some Play surfaces. Use a YouTube video URL. | Public or unlisted, embeddable, not private or age-restricted, with ads disabled. Show real experience in the first 10 seconds and aim for at least 80% representative footage; add captions and localization. Avoid ranking, discount, and call-to-action claims. XR video has separate formats and placements. |

Assets can appear across Play surfaces and device form factors, where they may be cropped or shown side by side. Check the actual supported surfaces—phone, tablet/Chromebook, TV, car, Wear OS, or XR—instead of reviewing only a phone preview. By submitting assets, developers grant Google publicity rights described in section 6 of the [Developer Distribution Agreement](../developer-distribution-agreement/developer-distribution-agreement.en-US.md); confirm you have the rights needed for that license.

## Pre-release checklist

1. Compare each title, short/full description, developer identity, icon, screenshot, feature graphic, and video with the app version's real functionality.
2. Check character counts and file specifications; verify localization, crop-safe areas, asset rights, and alt text.
3. Search for language such as “free,” “discount,” “number one,” “best,” “official,” and “download” that can create promotional or misleading claims; inspect badges, logos, and implied endorsements in images.
4. Align target audience, content rating, child appeal, ads, and asset style. If children are in the audience, complete the applicable Families and data-protection work too.
5. Preview each supported device form factor, including large screens, Wear OS, TV, cars, and XR; these display and specification rules can change.
