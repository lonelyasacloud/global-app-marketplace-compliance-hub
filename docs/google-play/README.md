# Google Play / Google Play 应用商店

本目录整理 Google Play Developer Program Policies、Play Console 操作、Data Safety、权限申报、计费和 Android 发布要求。

## Priority reading path / 推荐阅读顺序

1. User Data and Data Safety
2. Sensitive permissions and declaration workflows
3. Account deletion and privacy disclosures
4. Content, UGC, child safety and age restrictions
5. Payments, subscriptions and alternative billing programs
6. Target API, release tracks and publishing readiness
7. Enforcement, remediation and appeals
8. Regional distribution and policy deadlines

## Source hierarchy / 来源层级

以当前 Google Play Policy Center、Android Developers 和 Play Console Help 为准。Android API 或开发指南不自动等于 Play eligibility；文档必须区分平台政策义务与工程实现建议。

[Google Play Policy Center](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/)
[Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/)
[Android Developers](https://developer.android.com/)

## Snapshot note / 快照说明

首批内容基于 2026-10-24 Google Play Knowledge Base 快照。快照中的链接审计和访问状态描述当时的核验结果，发布前仍需回到当前官方页面复核。
