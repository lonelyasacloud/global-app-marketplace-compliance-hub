# Child safety regional reporting directory

> **Import note (Google batch 3).** The body is the paraphrased summary from the already separated 2026-10-24 Google Play knowledge-base snapshot (`play.child-safety-regional-reporting`, reviewed 2026-10-24). It is not a verbatim copy of an official page. The English official source is the source of truth. `kb_checked_at` is the review date recorded in the snapshot source file; this import did not re-open the live official pages. Phrases such as “this knowledge base,” “this library,” and “local topic” describe coverage inside that snapshot, not the set of pages imported into this repository. Relative snapshot links to topics, link graphs, ledgers, or CSVs that are not in this repository are kept as text mentions. Relative links that target the Google batch 1 and 2 topics or these nine batch 3 topics point at the hub files. The primary source recorded in the snapshot is the English Google Search Help article, opened in full on 2026-10-24. The snapshot does not record a checked Chinese official page for that directory. The Chinese hub text is the snapshot’s editorial paraphrase. Failed fetches of individual regional-organization sites are access limits, not policy changes, and no future-effective Play date is recorded. This directory is an operational route-finding aid. It is not a Google Play policy obligation, and inclusion of a third-party site does not establish that every Play developer is subject to that jurisdiction.

**Reviewed: October 24, 2026.** Google's Search Help article [Report child sexual abuse imagery](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/148666?hl=en) says that child sexual abuse material can be reported to NCMEC or to the listed regional organizations. If a child may be in immediate danger, contact the police. If a country is not listed, contact INHOPE. The page lists 48 country/region entries, 45 of which have linked regional organization websites; NCMEC and INHOPE are separate cross-regional routes. Some country entries list only an organization or phone number, with no website link.

**Relationship to Google Play:** Play's Child Endangerment/Child Safety Standards require in-scope developers to follow applicable law and maintain a CSAM response/reporting process. This Google Search Help directory is a public route-finding aid. It does not specify one reporting recipient, covered legal entity, trigger, or deadline for every Play developer. Verify the applicable law for the relevant service and location, then follow the organization’s current instructions.

## Coverage and follow-up

- All 45 regional website links shown in the Google article were attempted: 26 returned readable page text (including redirect destinations), two returned only a landing or empty response, one yielded a single unreadable line, one returned partial text, and 15 failed because of internal errors, HTTP errors, timeouts, or cache misses.
- `Body readable` does not verify that an organization still has the stated role, its official authority, eligibility, processing deadlines, privacy practices, or every site link. Check the organization's current page and local law.
- This is a snapshot of a Google Search Help directory; country coverage and links can change. NCMEC and INHOPE are followed separately in the child-safety topic, but every INHOPE member's full page and descendants have not been audited.
- These third-party reporting sites are operational resources, not additional Google Play policy duties. Their inclusion does not establish that every app developer is legally subject to that jurisdiction.
