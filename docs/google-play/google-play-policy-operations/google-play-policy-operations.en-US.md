# Google Play Policy Operations Control Register

It is not a verbatim copy of an official page. The English official source is the source of truth. `kb_checked_at` is the review date recorded in the snapshot source file; this import did not re-open the live official pages. Phrases such as “this knowledge base,” “this library,” and “local topic” describe coverage inside that snapshot, not the set of pages imported into this repository. Relative snapshot links to topics, link graphs, ledgers, or CSVs that are not in this repository are kept as text mentions. Relative links that target Google batch 1–8 topics or these eight batch 9 topics point at the hub files. Reviewed 2026-10-24. The 2026-11-01 extensions, the 2026-12-01 US External Content Links download report, 2027-02-01 16 KB page size, 2027-03-01 program dates, 2027-03-15 India merchant KYC, 2027-03-31 Japan external payments, 2027-09-30 Level Up, and the 2027/2028 Billing Library dates are not that 2027-01-27 preview. `MANAGE_CONTACTS` has no calendar date and is not rewritten as 2027-01-27. **Purpose:** Turn policy requirements into controls that can be checked before release, during updates, and in ongoing operations. This is a paraphrased navigation and checklist, not a complete translation or legal advice.

## 1. Coverage boundary

The official [Developer Policy Center](https://play.google/developer-content-policy/) currently lists 51 policy-topic destinations. All 51 destinations and topic summaries are catalogued in the [Policy Center entry](../google-play-developer-policy-center/google-play-developer-policy-center.en-US.md) and link graph (snapshot: `google-play-policy-link-graph-en.md`, not imported in this repository). The current consolidated policy text was used to check the controls below. Actual release obligations also come from the [Developer Distribution Agreement](https://play.google.com/developer-distribution-agreement.html), Play Console requirements and declarations, regional billing programs, and program-specific terms.

There is no closed, static set of “all Google Play-related policies”: policies change and refer to local laws, account-specific Play Console forms, payment program terms, Android APIs, and specialized programs. This register covers the public, generally applicable policy framework and major operational guidance verifiable as of the review date. It does not claim to reproduce every country's laws, signed-in page, or every page on the Android documentation site. Assess each app against its countries, audience, functions, device form factors, and account tasks.

## 3. Current deadline snapshot

The actionable timeline below reflects official pages checked on October 24, 2026. The live Policy Deadlines page has eight rows: three changes due August 26, 2026 and one registration date due September 30, 2026 have passed; four changes take effect January 27, 2027. The table below also tracks related Android, billing-program, and regional-merchant milestones. Policy Deadlines uses `YYYY-MM-DD`; verify program eligibility, declarations, and account-specific extension status in the linked official page or Play Console.

**Public-source date discrepancy (unresolved):** The April, June, and July 2026 Play Developers Newsletters each label the Contacts/location reminder “Starting October 28, 2026,” while the dedicated [Policy Deadlines](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/table/12921780?hl=en) table, [policy preview](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16909972?hl=en), and Contacts minimum-scope Help page list **January 27, 2027** as the formal deadline. I reopened the June newsletter and dedicated table on October 24; the table still has only the January 27 Contacts/Location policy rows. A separate April 15, 2026 Android Developers blog says Contacts/location-specific Play Console pre-review checks are planned to start **October 27, 2026**. The October 5 [official Play Community compliance reminder](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/thread/471472559/mandatory-steps-for-contacts-permissions-policy-compliance-by-january-27-2027?hl=en-GB) is now readable in Google's official search index: apps targeting API 37+ and requesting `READ_CONTACTS` must submit a declaration, request a 30-day self-extension, or remove the permission by January 27; the directly opened post still does not expose body links. Public sources do not explain the relationship among October 27, October 28, and January 27. This register keeps all three dates and does not infer that October 28 supersedes the formal deadline. For apps that need only occasional/selective contact access, use October 28 as a conservative migration milestone; for broad access, prepare the formal declaration and use January 27 as the current published mandatory date. The early-migration advice is a risk-control inference. Recheck the live policy and actual Play Console after rollout. The three newsletter reviews are in policy-link graph §4.92 (snapshot: `google-play-policy-link-graph-en.md`, not imported in this repository); the Contacts reminder and child-link status are in §4.133 (snapshot: `google-play-policy-link-graph-en.md`, not imported in this repository).

### Enforcement notices and appeals

When a rejection, removal, suspension, limited-visibility/region, or account-termination notice arrives, confirm the affected app, action, policy clause, and response deadline in Policy status and the notice email; review the rest of the account catalog and third-party SDKs too. Fix the issue, submit the update or one appropriate appeal through the path stated in Console/the notice, and retain build, test, permission/data inventory, reviewer-access, and remediation evidence. Account-termination appeals have a 180-day window from January 28, 2026; routes and deadlines for app-level decisions depend on the individual notice. Play Protect sideload warnings, reports about other apps, and EEA mediation are separate workflows; see the [bilingual enforcement and appeals topic](../enforcement-and-appeals/enforcement-and-appeals.en-US.md).
