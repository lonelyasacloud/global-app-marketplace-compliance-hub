## Relationship to the DDA and API terms

- The Play Console Terms apply to individual users of Console. A user must be at least 18 and of legal age to form a binding contract in their country. These Terms are additional to the [Developer Distribution Agreement (DDA)](../developer-distribution-agreement/developer-distribution-agreement.en-US.md); users must accept the DDA to use Play Console.
- A person accepting the DDA for an employer/entity must have authority to bind it. The Console Terms define an Account Owner as the person who initially created the developer account and Console Users as the other individuals on that account.
- The Console Terms, the DDA, and—when Developer APIs are used—the [Google Play Developer API Terms](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/terms) form the legal agreement governing Console use. The Console Terms state a precedence rule for conflicts among these documents. API users must also follow the Developer API Terms and the general [Google APIs Terms](https://developers.google.com/terms). These are separate documents, not interchangeable versions of the DDA or Developer Program Policies.

## Usage information and account auditing

Google associates Play Console and Developer API usage information with the developer account. Examples include Console pages visited and data viewed, actions using Console features, API actions, the login email, IP addresses, and timestamps. The Terms allow Google to share this information with the Account Owner to manage the account, audit activity, and monitor potential abuse. When apps transfer to a new developer account, related usage information is associated with that account and may be shared with its Account Owner. The Terms allow retention while the associated developer account remains active, even if a user's Console access or login is deactivated.

This relates to Console user permissions and Activity log. Account owners should use least-privilege roles and consider operation-history visibility when granting/revoking access or transferring an account. See [Play Console users and permissions](../play-console-user-permissions/play-console-user-permissions.en-US.md).

## Restrictions, changes, and termination

- Do not use Console beyond its intended functionality; to engage in, promote, or encourage illegal activity or abuse; to disable, interfere with, reverse engineer, or circumvent Console; or to access another Google service in violation of that service's terms.
- Google may change, discontinue, or limit Console features. Changes to the Terms are notified in Console and to the contact email. For existing users, changes generally take effect on the stated date no sooner than 30 days after posting; changes for new Console functions or required by law may take effect immediately. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
- If a user disagrees with a change, the Terms identify stopping Console use as the exclusive remedy. Violations may result in termination of Console access; to the extent permitted by law, Google may also terminate access for other reasons with 30 days' notice.
- The governing law and jurisdiction in the DDA also apply to claims relating to the Console Terms. API users must separately read the API ToS.

## Boundary with other Play requirements

The Console Terms govern individuals' use of Console, Console usage information, and service changes. The DDA governs the contract for distributing products through Play. Developer Program Policies govern app/account compliance. Developer API Terms add purpose restrictions to specific APIs. Accepting Console Terms or the DDA does not mean an app has passed review or waive policy declarations, privacy duties, or local laws.
