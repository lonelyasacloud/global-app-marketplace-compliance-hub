# Google Play Policy Archive and Historical Version Index

On the review date, the official [Policy Archive index](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13386702?hl=en) listed 56 dated Developer Program Policy PDF snapshots, from 2026-08-26 back to 2016-03. This page records each displayed date, official PDF target, and fetch status.

## How to use this archive

- These PDFs are historical policy-text snapshots for reconstructing the text relevant to a past review, appeal, or contract dispute. They are not current policy or current deadlines.
- For the reason for a change and the developer actions, consult the [announcement index](../google-play-policy-announcements/google-play-policy-announcements.en-US.md). Current requirements are governed by the live [Developer Program Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18258653) and the applicable topic pages.
- The index uses full dates for newer versions and month-only labels for older ones; month-only labels are preserved without inventing a day.
- All 56 PDF targets have been mapped: 39 returned PDF bodies; of the remaining 17, two timed out, four exceeded the web reader size limit, and 11 were not accessible through this browser tool. A successful fetch confirms access only; this review did not summarize all 56 versions or recursively inspect links inside every PDF. The audit ledger records access and this remaining content-analysis gap separately.
- To support migration, this index stores dates, stable source links, and paraphrased usage notes; it does not reproduce the full official PDFs.
