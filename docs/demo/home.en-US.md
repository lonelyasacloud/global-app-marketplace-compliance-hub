## How to use a topic page

Each topic exposes:

- the platform, language, authority, and current status;
- the knowledge-base review date and any effective date;
- official source links;
- release evidence to prepare;
- related topics and the coverage boundary.

The library is an operational summary and navigation layer. It does not replace current Apple, Google, regulatory, or legal sources and does not guarantee approval.

## Evidence rule

Official source first. Review date visible. Current requirements separated from future-dated changes. Partial coverage and unavailable source pages disclosed.
